# Personal Knowledge Base

This is your personal knowledge base, maintained collaboratively with Oracle.

## Directory Structure

```
├── README.md              # This file
├── DISPATCH.md            # Scheduled tasks, milestones, and proactive alerts
├── inbox/                 # Inbound review inbox where Oracle stages newly learned insights
├── entities/              # Structured facts about people, hardware, servers, and life entities
│   ├── me.md              # Your core profile, goals, habits, and background
│   └── infrastructure.md  # Your devices, network, homelab nodes, and services
└── mind/                  # Your preferences, principles, and guidelines
    └── preferences.md     # How you like things done, communication style, etc.
```

## How Oracle Interacts with Your Knowledge Base

1. **Autonomous Knowledge Retrieval**: When you chat on Discord, Oracle references relevant notes here.
2. **Post-Turn Learning**: After conversational turns, Oracle synthesizes new facts and stages them into `inbox/` as dated `pending-review` notes for you to vet.
3. **Dispatches**: Tasks with future due dates in `DISPATCH.md` are monitored by Oracle and proactively delivered to your Discord DMs.
4. **Curiosity Engine**: Open questions documented in your entity files can trigger thoughtful, proactive check-ins at comfortable cadences.
